# Shot list for your own demo video (about 2:30, your screen + your voice)

Record with the Xbox Game Bar (Win+G, then the record button) or OBS. Speak naturally; one take per shot is
fine, and you cut them together after. Before you record, turn off notifications, close private tabs, and set
**Settings → Nudge after** to 90 s so the drift moment doesn't take long.

| # | Length | Show | Say (your words) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:15 | Your face or a messy screen with 30 tabs | "I have ADHD. Every focus app waits for me to open it. I never do. So I built one that speaks first." |
| 2 | 0:15 | Double-click Nudgling. It wakes and asks "What are you working on?" Type *Banafah verification* | "It lives at the bottom of my screen. First it asks what I'm doing, and splits it into tiny steps." |
| 3 | 0:25 | Work a moment, then open YouTube and wait ~90 s. The "!", the "hm?", the bubble | "When I drift, it doesn't lecture me. It names my quest and one thing I can do in two minutes." |
| 4 | 0:15 | Ignore it twice more → the bomb across the screen | "And if I ignore it... it gets a little dramatic." |
| 5 | 0:20 | In a Claude chat: "fix the checkout bug". Then "also add a dark mode" → Claude answers with the scope warning | "It also watches my scope. I start a bug fix and suddenly I'm adding features. It catches that too." |
| 6 | 0:15 | WordPress: an Elementor page, then open Orders → "Side task?" | "Same site, different job: it notices when I wander inside my own work." |
| 7 | 0:20 | Click the creature → FOCUS 25. Fast-forward the ring; "FLOW ×3"; then gold rain (or show the screenshot) | "Focus pays: a combo that grows, gold rain for a perfect session, things it can wear." |
| 8 | 0:15 | HUD: the AI card (Llama on this PC), then a Zoom window (it goes quiet) | "Meta's Llama runs right on my PC, so my screen never leaves it. In calls it stays silent." |
| 9 | 0:10 | The GitHub page, AGENTS.md | "Give the link to any AI agent, and it sets itself up." |
| 10 | 0:10 | The creature cheering | "It's not a productivity app. It's a body double that speaks first." |

The full-screen moments (bomb, rage, gold rain, confetti) also exist as real screenshots in `docs/screens/`, if
